Welcome

Fue un placer conocerlos. Thank you for showing up on Monday — and for bringing your words. I heard beautiful stories during the session, and in the breakout rooms you shared pieces of your own relationship with Spanish: how you learned it, with whom, why it matters to you now. Those conversations are the program.

What happened on Monday was something particular. It was a conversation between people who, for different reasons, carry Spanish inside them — as a first language, as a heritage, as something being built right now.

In the coming sessions we'll explore new themes and try different dynamics — other ways of organizing breakouts, other ways of entering the culture. This is only the beginning.

One space, once a month. Twelve months. We begin.

This month

We looked at Hispanic cultures through a specific lens: words.

Not vocabulary in the textbook sense. Words that mean something to us — because of a memory, because of how they sound, because they carry a piece of where we come from, or where we want to go.

Each of you brought one. Some of you brought more than one, in the breakouts.

A moment to keep

What stood out — beyond the words themselves — was what emerged in the breakouts.

People talking about their grandparents. About the Spanish they learned at the kitchen table. About the Spanish they're learning now, as adults, by their own choice. About why any of this matters.

That's the whole point of Hablemos. The words are the excuse. What's shared around them is the program.

De qué hablamos este mes

Miramos las culturas hispanas a través de una ventana específica: las palabras.

No el vocabulario de los libros. Palabras que significan algo para nosotros — por un recuerdo, por cómo suenan, por lo que cargan de dónde venimos, o de dónde queremos ir.

Cada uno trajo una. Algunos trajeron más de una, en los grupos pequeños.

Un momento para guardar

Lo que sobresalió — más allá de las palabras — fue lo que apareció en los grupos pequeños.

Gente hablando de sus abuelos. Del español que aprendieron en la mesa de la cocina. Del español que están aprendiendo ahora, de adultos, por decisión propia. De por qué todo esto importa.

Eso es Hablemos. Las palabras son la excusa. Lo que se comparte alrededor es el programa.
